Tighten the day your two live levers

Prices and weekly days are locked this season, so the only cost you control is time: mowing closer to the crew's own proven pace, and cutting the dead time between stops. Route order is already tight (re-sequencing saves under $40/week), and at ~6 mph implied between stops, most of that "drive" time is transition, not driving. So this is where the money is.
